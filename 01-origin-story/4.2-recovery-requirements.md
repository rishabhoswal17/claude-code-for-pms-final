## How priority is set

Each story is scored on the same urgency scale used in the sentiment analysis, plus how well it
fits the roadmap.

| Input | What it means | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Reach** | How many people raised it | Interviews (out of 4), tickets (count and responders) |
| **Harm** | What it costs a responder or handler | Sentiment: very negative → mild |
| **Data** | Whether the database backs it up | Pings and tickets |
| **Roadmap fit** | Can it ride on something already planned? | Q3 roadmap |

| Priority | Rule | Roadmap effect |
|---|---|---|
| **P0 (now)** | Urgency 4–5 and tied to a root cause | Uses items already committed to Q3. No new roadmap lines. |
| **P1 (next)** | Urgency 3, or stops this happening again | Small slices or non-roadmap work (docs, reporting) |
| **P2 (later)** | Urgency 2, real but not harmful | Normal defect or backlog queue |
| **P3 (parked)** | Not related to 4.2, or weak signal | No change. Logged for the owning team. |

**Guardrail: no full revert of 4.2.** The change to who gets pinged was a long-standing customer
ask (Priya's handoff). The fixes below target the answer window and the scoring rule, not the
decision to weight distance more.

## Initiative 1: Give every responder a fair shot at callouts again

*Fixes root causes 1 and 2. Vehicle: "Ping timeout tuning", already committed to Q3 and only
partly delivered (4.2 shipped a flat cut, not tuning).*

**Goal:** missed pings back near the pre-4.2 level (about 4 a week, from about 28), and no
responder below half their usual pings without a reason.

### Epic 1.1: An answer window people can actually meet

Evidence: 15 tickets across 11 of 16 responders, 3 of 4 interviews · Negative · Urgency 4/5

| # | User story | Priority | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1.1 | As a **responder**, I want enough time to reach my phone and answer, so I don't lose jobs I was about to take. | **P0** | Missed pings return to within 2× the pre-4.2 weekly level. The window value is set from data (1.1.2), not picked by hand. |
| 1.1.2 | As the **PM**, I want to see how long responders actually take to tap "take", so the window is based on real behavior. | **P0** | A distribution of ping-to-tap times from before and after 4.2, shared with Marcus and Wen. |
| 1.1.3 | As a **responder**, I want the ping to show how much time is left, so I know whether to rush. | P2 | Countdown shown on the phone ping. Sofia to scope. |

### Epic 1.2: A missed ping shouldn't bury a responder
