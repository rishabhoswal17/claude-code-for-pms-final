Bringing quiet responders back, fairly and visibly

You asked what we'd build instead, seen by the people it happens to, before anyone touches the code.

Short answer: change the rule that quietly pushed some responders to the back of the line, then let responders and handlers see when someone is going quiet, so it never runs for three months unnoticed again.

What happened

90s → 60s4.2 shortens the answer window

Responders get less time to answer a job offer (a "ping").

~4 → ~30Missed pings a week

The four hit hardest missed 44–63% in release week.

0.84 → 0Each miss counted as a "no"

The penalty never fades. Farlight's score hit zero by 28 Aug.

12 → ~1Pings a week for the cut-off four

Farlight, The Undertow, Vesper, Meteor Mite. Their areas' jobs went to busy outsiders.

45 openHandlers find out late

"Phone never goes off" tickets arrive 1–2 weeks after. Nobody owns the numbers.

Overall, pings taken fell from 76.6% to 64.0%. The "miss = no" and "never fades" rules are older than 4.0. 4.2 didn't create them, it woke them up. Turn-downs actually fell after 4.2. Misses decided who got cut off.
